BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Clear answers.
Better decisions.

Which package should I choose?+

Start with the number of pages and the functionality you need. Launch covers a straightforward presence; Growth and Premium give individual services more space. For ecommerce or complex integrations, request an Advanced scope review.

Are hosting and domain fees included?+

Domain connection assistance is included. Domain registration, hosting, premium plugins, booking tools, payment processing, and other third-party costs are separate unless your written scope says otherwise.

What counts as a revision round?+

One consolidated batch of changes to the agreed design. New pages, major new functionality, or a complete redesign after approval may be additional scope.

What does basic SEO include?+

Appropriate titles, descriptions, page structure, and technical basics. It is not an ongoing SEO campaign and does not guarantee rankings, traffic, leads, or revenue.

What happens after payment?+

Stripe redirects website customers to the existing package-specific intake questionnaire. Submit your business details and usable, authorized materials so production can begin.
